**Subject:** Yle Finnish News Archive 2011–2024, scrambled, Korp: release status and annotation pipeline

Hello,

I am using the Korp corpora `YLENEWS_FI_2011_S` … `YLENEWS_FI_2024_S`
(*Yle Finnish News Archive 2011–2024, scrambled, Korp*,
urn:nbn:fi:lb-2025112103) for a research comparison of noun frequencies
across years. I would be grateful for answers to the following questions.

**Release status**

1. Your announcement of 11 December 2025 described the 2022–2024 extension
   (urn:nbn:fi:lb-2025110403 and lb-2025102201) as a release candidate, in
   which small changes might be made without notice. Is the extension still
   a release candidate, or has it been declared a stable release? If so,
   when?
2. Has the Korp data for 2022–2024 changed since 11 December 2025? Korp
   reports "Updated" 2025-11-20/21 for these corpora.

**Annotation pipeline**

3. Which parser or annotation pipeline, and which version, annotated the
   lemmas, POS and MSD of `YLENEWS_FI_2011_S` … `_2018_S`
   (urn:nbn:fi:lb-2019121004) and of `YLENEWS_FI_2019_S` … `_2021_S`
   (urn:nbn:fi:lb-2022032201)? The metadata of the 2022–2024 part names
   TDPP-LBF (urn:nbn:fi:lb-2024021503); the older parts name no tool.
4. The combined record urn:nbn:fi:lb-2025112103 lists TDPP-LBF as the
   annotation tool for 2011–2024. Were the 2011–2021 parts re-annotated with
   TDPP-LBF, or does that entry refer only to the 2022–2024 extension?
5. Can the lemma, POS and MSD annotation of the three parts be considered
   comparable for counting noun lemmas across years? Are there known
   differences, for example in tokenisation, lemmatisation of unknown words,
   or the handling of proper nouns?

**Known annotation behaviour** (for information; a reply is optional)

6. We noticed, consistently across years, that *joista* after a comma is
   often tagged as the noun *joki*; *kuin* is sometimes tagged as the noun
   *kuu* (plural instructive); and the bare noun *sää* "weather" is mostly
   tagged as a pronoun. Are these known issues?

Thank you.
